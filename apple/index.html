The problem

The reality

Email Overload

Newsletters, vet appointments, caregiver updates, event invitations, board communications. Critical information buried in an endless stream of messages from teachers, coaches, doctors, and committee members.

Calendar Chaos

Multiple kids, aging parents, grooming schedules, galas, board meetings. One missed update means a scramble to rearrange your day or not being present when it matters most — at home and at work.

Mental Load

“Is there school tomorrow?” “When’s Dad’s cardiology appointment?” “Did we confirm the speaker for the fundraiser?” Questions that interrupt your focus and fragment your day.

No Infrastructure at Home

You have EAs, project managers, and systems keeping your professional life on track. But your personal life — just as complex, just as consequential — runs on memory and group texts.

Exhale changes that. One AI system that runs your home life leaner and smarter than most enterprise operations.

How it works

Built around your life

  1. 1

    We map your world together

    You set up a few simple automations so your important emails and updates flow to one central place. We take it from there — connecting your communication channels and configuring Exhale around your life.

  2. 2

    Ask anything, anytime

    Message Exhale like you’d text a trusted assistant. Plain language questions get instant answers pulled from your own information — emails, schedules, and websites all in one place.

  3. 3

    We keep it running

    Your life changes — new schools, new caregivers, new commitments. We update Exhale continuously so it always reflects how you actually live. You never manage the system. We do.
